02 Device Permissions & What We Access

To perform its core photo management and gamified cleaning features, Streak Clean requests specific Android system permissions. Here is a transparent breakdown of each permission and its exact purpose:

android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+
Enables the app to query and display your local photos within the swipe deck. Photos are loaded directly from your device's local storage and are never transmitted over the network.
android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 12 and below
Provides backward compatibility on older Android versions to inspect local camera roll photos for local cleaning.
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Photo Deletion
Required on supported Android versions to move photos marked for removal to your device's trash or delete them upon your explicit confirmation during the Batch Review step.
android.permission.ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION EXIF Metadata
Permits reading embedded local EXIF metadata (such as timestamps and photo details) strictly on your device to organize photo cards by date and size. Location data is never transmitted, logged, or shared.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional Alerts
Allows Streak Clean to send local reminders configured by you (e.g. daily streak reminders). These notifications are generated locally by the operating system—no remote push notification tokens or server connections are involved.
android.permission.INTERNET Network Access
Used strictly to load remote vector avatar illustrations (DiceBear API) and to open external web links (such as this Privacy Policy or support pages) when triggered by you. No photo data or personal analytics are ever sent over this connection.
android.permission.VIBRATE & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Utilities
Provides tactile haptic feedback during swipe gestures and reschedules your local streak notification alarms if your device reboots.

03 Information Collection & Third-Party SDKs

Zero Third-Party Trackers: Streak Clean does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or behavior profiling networks.

  • No Personal Accounts: Streak Clean does not require account creation, registration, email sign-up, or social logins.
  • No Advertising Networks: We do not show third-party ads (no Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, Facebook Audience Network, or similar).
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not track your clicks, screen views, or swipe habits via third-party analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel).
  • Local Game Progression: Your streak counter, level progress, unlocked badges, fruit persona, and total freed storage count are stored strictly on your device using encrypted/local key-value storage (MMKV).

04 Photo Deletion & User Control

You have absolute control over which photos are kept or deleted:

  • Swiping left merely stages a photo for review; no files are deleted until you inspect the Batch Review summary.
  • When you confirm deletion, Streak Clean invokes Android's standard MediaStore trash/delete API. Depending on your Android version and manufacturer settings, deleted media is typically placed into the system Gallery Trash/Bin for 30 days before permanent deletion.
  • Streak Clean does not maintain backups, copies, or shadow archives of any deleted photos.

05 Data Retention & Storage

Because all application state (preferences, streaks, XP) is stored locally on your device:

  • Clearing the app's cache or data through your Android system settings instantly removes all stored preferences.
  • Uninstalling Streak Clean permanently erases all locally saved game metrics and settings.

07 Security

The security of your memories is our highest priority. By deliberately choosing an offline-first, zero-upload architecture, we eliminate the security risks associated with cloud data breaches, interceptable network transmissions, and remote database vulnerabilities. Your photos remain under your physical device control at all times.

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Deletion: You can purge all app records at any moment by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the app.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
